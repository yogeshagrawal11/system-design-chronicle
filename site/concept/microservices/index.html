🧱 Microservices

Split a big application into small, independently deployable services that each own one business capability and its data.

A microservice architecture builds an application as a set of small services such as *Catalog*, *Cart*, *Orders* and *Payments*. Each one runs in its own process, owns its own database, is deployed on its own schedule and talks to the others only through APIs (REST/gRPC) or events. The payoff is…

✅ Checksums, Hashes & Merkle Trees

Small fingerprints that catch silent corruption, identify content, and let replicas find differences cheaply.

Data gets corrupted silently: a bit flips in RAM, a NIC or router mangles a packet, a disk returns the wrong sector, firmware writes to the wrong place. Nothing throws an error; you just read back different bytes. A checksum is a small fixed-size value computed from the data. Store or send it…

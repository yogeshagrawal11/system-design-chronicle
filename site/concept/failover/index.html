🔀 Failover

Automatically switch traffic to a standby when the primary dies, without two primaries and without losing data.

Failover is the act of moving work from a failed component to a healthy redundant one. Every failover has the same four stages: detect that the primary is gone (heartbeats, health checks), decide who takes over (an election or an external arbiter), fence the old primary so it cannot keep acting…

Concept

🟢 Availability

The fraction of time users can actually use the system, measured in nines.

Availability is the share of time (or of requests) for which the system does its job for users: availability = uptime / (uptime + downtime), or for request-based SLOs successful requests / valid requests. It is usually quoted in nines: 99.9% (three nines) allows ~43 minutes of downtime a month;…

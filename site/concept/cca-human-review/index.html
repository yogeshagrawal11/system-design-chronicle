🔍 Human Review Workflows and Confidence Calibration

Do not let a 97% headline hide an 85% segment: measure by document type and field, calibrate confidence against labeled data, sample what you auto-accept, and spend reviewer time where it matters.

An extraction pipeline that is "97% accurate" can still be failing badly somewhere. If typed invoices are 70% of volume at 99% accuracy, a 10% slice of handwritten forms can sit at 85% and the overall number barely moves. Aggregate metrics mask segment failures, so accuracy has to be validated…

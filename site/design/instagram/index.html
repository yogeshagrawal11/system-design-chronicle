📸 Instagram (Photo Sharing & Feed)

Upload a photo once, show it in a few hundred million ranked feeds, and count a million likes a minute.

Instagram is a media pipeline bolted onto a social feed. The write path takes a 12 MP photo or a video from a phone on a shaky network, stores it durably and processes it into many sizes and formats. The read path must show every user a fresh, ranked feed in ~100 ms, which means precomputing…

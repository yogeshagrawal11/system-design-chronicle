🛒 Dynamo

Amazon's Highly Available Key-value Store: always accept the write, sort out conflicts later.

Dynamo is the key-value store Amazon built so that services like the shopping cart could keep accepting writes even while disks fail, servers crash and data centres get cut off from each other. Its guiding choice: when the CAP trade-off bites, give up immediate consistency to stay available.…
